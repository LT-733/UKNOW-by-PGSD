# UKNOW-by-PGSD

UKNOW is a university lookup website that gives statistical insights on programs based on the GPA average of admission.

To use UKNOW, go to [https://uknow-by-pgsd.vercel.app/](https://uknow-by-pgsd.vercel.app/) and provide the search engine your desired program name, the university of your choosing, and your current estimated GPA. The website will provide a list of results including a risk rating based on your GPA and the average admissions GPA. Click on any program name to find a page tailored for said program, which includes an accurate program description, a link to the program, and trends of the program's average GPA.


*UKNOW is a project made by TEAM PGSD, with the primary goal to help guide the grade 12s of Canada through their search for their secondary education.*
